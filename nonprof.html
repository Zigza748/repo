About Arte Madrid
Events

At Arte Madrid Events, we believe every celebration holds a story worth preserving. Founded by Haiza Madrid, Arte Madrid Events specializes in multicultural weddings, milestone celebrations, and cinematic storytelling that captures the meaning behind life's most important moments.

With a background in hospitality, entertainment, and business strategy, Arte Madrid Events blend creative direction with production expertise to design celebrations that are both beautiful and deeply personal.

From weddings to community celebrations and documentary films, our mission is simple:

To turn every story into art.
